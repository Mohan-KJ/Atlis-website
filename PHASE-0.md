# Phase 0 design review

## Delivered

- Three Home hero directions, each with a supporting content section.
- Desktop mockup proportion at 1366 px and mobile mockup proportion at 390 px.
- Explicit concept and trade-off for each direction.
- Sample-data labeling on the product surfaces.
- Claims register for factual statements rendered in the review board.
- Route manifest for `/` and `/design-directions`.

## Decision gate

The owner must choose **A / Product-first**, **B / Editorial and clinical calm**, or **C / Claim journey** before production pages are implemented.

## Intentionally omitted

No production marketing pages, pilot form backend, owner-confirmed statuses, legal entity details, security claims, insurer pilot statuses, testimonials, logos, customer names, approved screenshots, or unsupported performance/compliance claims are rendered in this checkpoint.
